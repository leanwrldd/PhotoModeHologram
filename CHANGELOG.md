# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Added optional Smart Camera 2.0 support. After you close camera mode, the build viewport shows where you left the free camera until you stop building.

## 1.0.0

- First release. Opening camera mode no longer puts the build gun away or discards your hologram.
- The hologram locks in place while camera mode is open, and a hologram you locked yourself stays locked afterwards.
